7 Cures to a Lean Purse

Summary and Alignment to my Life

The first cure introduces the fundamental principle of wealth building: to begin, you must increase your savings. Arkad instructs his 100 students on this method, advising them to save 1 coin for every 10 they earn while utilizing the remaining 9. Gradually, this practice will lead to a fuller purse, fulfilling their financial aspirations. I consider this an essential foundation for financial growth and effective money management.

The second cure reveals an important reality: despite their varying responsibilities and incomes, they all share a common challenge, the lean purse. It is crucial for them to distinguish between needs and wants. Arkad teaches them the importance of budgeting by creating a priority list, addressing essential expenses before indulging in desires. I appreciate the example Arkad provided to the young student; it resonates with my own journey as I prepare for the workforce. I must also hold myself accountable for my spending. For instance, if I had tasks like paying bills and buying a new phone, I would ensure that paying the bills takes precedence before treating myself to a new phone.

The third cure emphasizes the importance of wise investments. Arkad shared his experience of losing all his initial investment but eventually achieving a return when he engaged in loans with Aggar. This aligns with the fourth law of Gold, which warns that the gold accumulated through saving can be easily lost without a proper understanding of the business or operations in which one invests. The fourth cure advocates for smart investing, advising that one should only invest where the principal is secure and the returns are reasonable. It stresses the need to avoid risky schemes and seek guidance from knowledgeable advisors.

The fifth cure emphasizes the significance of viewing one's home as a valuable investment. This concept resonated with me because, rather than renting and losing money each month, owning a home allows for managing costs effectively by only spending on what is used. Over time, the home builds equity, and with proper budgeting, I can maximize its benefits. The sixth cure encourages us to prepare for the future by setting aside funds, reminding me of my parents' teachings about the importance of saving and investing for what lies ahead. Additionally, it highlights the need for personal development in our fields to increase our earning potential. This aligns perfectly with the seventh cure, which stresses the continuous refinement of our skills and knowledge to enhance our capacity to earn more.

Takeaway: The main takeaway from this chapter is that we should emulate the wise men, not only by being like them but also by surrounding ourselves with individuals who possess wisdom. These wise minds can guide us along a path enriched with knowledge, where we learn from their experiences and refine our understanding through their teachings. We must embrace the joy found in the lengthy journey of learning, allowing us to grasp the fundamentals and understand the basics. Finally, we should avoid shortcuts, as true wisdom lies in following the wise and recognizing that such paths are not beneficial for investment.
