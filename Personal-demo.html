Critical Essay: LLMs as Tool, Not Crutch

"LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool, not a crutch."

As a student, I believe that large language models (LLMs) should enhance my thinking rather than replace it. Tools can enhance my capabilities, while relying too heavily on them can diminish my skills. If I let an LLM write my entire essay, I miss out on the valuable learning that comes from grappling with ideas. However, using it for brainstorming, grammar checks, or clarifying difficult concepts allows me to strengthen my own efforts.

    LLMs can significantly improve my learning experience when utilized for:
  • Generating practice questions
  • Summarizing complex readings
  • Providing constructive feedbacks on my drafts
  • Assisting in debugging code by clarifying errors

These tools should be employed only after I have made an attempt at the task myself. (Silva et al., 2026) It's essential for me to verify facts, cite sources, and consider whether I can explain the results without the assistance of AI.

In my academic journey, I treat LLMs as a tutor: I seek explanations and then strive to teach the concepts back to myself. (Jošt et al., 2024) I refrain from using them to compose my submissions, which helps me maintain integrity and develop genuine skills.

References:

  • Jošt, G., Taneski, V., & Karakatič, S. (2024). The impact of large language models on programming education and student learning outcomes. Applied Sciences, 14(10), 4115. https://doi.org/10.3390/app14104115.
  • Silva, D. F. E. R., Pinto, G., & Monteiro, D. (2026, July 31). Students' practices and skills in the LLM-Era: “You can't outsource the struggle and still get the skill.” arXiv.org. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29519.
